The author

Vivian Pontes

Author and illustrator.

Vivian Pontes is an Italian-Brazilian author who has lived in Portugal for several years. She is the author of Sacrifice of Eternis, a five-book series with four books published in English and Portuguese and the final volume scheduled for the first half of 2027.

She also works as an illustrator and creates the covers and part of the visual material for her books.
